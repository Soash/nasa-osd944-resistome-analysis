Study Protocol Description	Sampling materials consisted of  sterile polyester wipes (TX3211 Texwipe) moistened with 10ml of sterile molecular grade water and stored in 50ml conical bottom tubes (BD Falcon). Wipes were removed from tubes and handled with sterile gloves during sampling. Sampling consisted of folding wipes into quarters, wiping an area of approximately 1m^2 unidirectionally, folding to a new quarter of a wipe, wiping the same area in a different direction, and repeating for a total of 3 directions. Wipes were rolled up, placed back in the tubes, and transported to the lab for processing.	Sampling materials (sterile TX3211 (Texwipe) wipes moistened with molecular grade water) were extracted with PBS+Tween80, concentrated with Amicon concentrators, and extracted with Qiagen UCP pathogen miniprep kit. Zymo MMC samples were diluted from the D6306 Microbial Community DNA Standard. DNA was amplified with the Repli-G Midi Kit (Qiagen). Samples Zymo_MMC_Even_125pg, D1S5, D1S8, D1S10, D2S9, and NTC_1 were stopped at 12 hours of MDA reaction. Samples D2S2, D2S6, D2S7, D2S8, D2S10 ran Sampling materials (sterile TX3211 (Texwipe) wipes moistened with molecular grade water) were extracted with PBS+Tween80, concentrated with Amicon concentrators, and extracted with Qiagen UCP pathogen miniprep kit. Zymo MMC samples were diluted from the D6306 Microbial Community DNA Standard. DNA was amplified with the Repli-G Midi Kit (Qiagen). Samples Zymo_MMC_Even_125pg, D1S5, D1S8, D1S10, D2S9, and NTC_1 were stopped at 12 hours of MDA reaction. Samples D2S2, D2S6, D2S7, D2S8, D2S10 ran overnight (approximately 12 hours). All other samples were stopped at 2 hours of MDA reaction. MDA product was treated with T7 Endonuclease (New England Biolabs) and cleaned with the magnetic AXP beads from the Nanopore Rapid PCR Barcoding Kit 24 V14.	DNA libraries were prepared with the Nanopore Rapid PCR Barcoding Kit 24 V14 (Oxford Nanopore Technologies, Oxford, UK) following the manufacturer's protocol, using 14 PCR cycles. Library quality was assayed for each sample using the High Sensitivity D1000 DNA ScreenTape (Agilent Technologies, Santa Clara, CA) following the manufacturer's protocol. Sample libraries were pooled into 3 multiplex libraries. Multiplex library 1 contained samples Zymo_MMC_Even_125pg, D1S5, D1S8, D1S10, D2S9, and NTC_1. Multiplex library 2 contained samples Zymo_MMC_Even_2, D1S1, D2S1, and NTC_2. Multiplex library 3 contained all other samples. Sample libraries with Qubit values above the detection limit were pooled with eqimolar concentration and if sample libraries were below the Qubit detection limit, the maximum volume (49uL) was used. Multiplex library concentration was measured with Qubit 1x dsDNA HS assay (ThermoFisher Scientific). Final library loaded was 50 fmol.	DNA libraries were sequenced with the PromethION 2 Solo system and Minknow Software v25.03.9. 87 hour sequencing time; all other settings default	Fastq files were generated from the PromethION 2 Solo software using Dorado v7.8.3. Fastq files for each barcode were concatenated into one fastq file per barcode, then quality was assessed with Nanoplot v1.44.1 and reports were summarized with MultiQC v1.27.1.	"Note: The samples in this dataset were sequenced in 3 separate sequencing runs. The data was processed in parallel for all three sequencing runs and then combined in the QC reports and visualizations.
Quality assessment: Quality assessment of filtered, trimmed, blanks-removed (decontaminated) and human-removed reads was performed with Nanoplot v1.44.1 and reports were combined with MultiQC v1.27.1.
Pre-processing: Raw concatenated reads were filtered by quality (Phred quality greater than or equal to 8) and length (greater than or equal to 200bp) using filtlong v0.2.1. Adapters were then removed from the filtered reads using porechop v0.2.4. Quality controlled blank / negative control sample reads were assembled using Flye v2.9.5. Contaminant (blank) reads were removed from the samples by mapping quality filtered/trimmed reads to the assembled blank contigs using minimap2 v2.28. Then, unmapped/decontaminated reads were identified and output to decontaminated fastq files using samtools v1.20. After contaminant removal, human reads were removed using kraken2 v2.1.4 against a human genome reference database that was constructed from NCBI's RefSeq (GCF_000001405.39) GRCh38.p13. The database was constructed by running the kraken build command with the following parameter set --no-masking, --kmer-length 35 and --minimizer-length 31.
Read-based processing: Taxonomy assignment of decontaminated/human-removed reads was performed with Kraken2 v2.1.4 and Kaiju v1.10.1. The resulting assignments were visualized as krona plots using krona v2.8.1.
Assembly-based analysis: The decontaminated/human-removed reads were assembled with Flye v2.9.5 then polished using medaka v2.0.1. Genes were called with prodigal v2.6.3. Taxonomic classification of genes and contigs was performed with CAT v5.2.3. Functional annotation was done with KOFamScan v1.3.0. Reads were mapped to assembles with minimap2 v2.28, and coverage information was extracted for both reads and contigs with samtools v1.20 and bbmap v38.86. Binning of contigs was performed with metabat2 v2.15. Bins were summarized with bit v1.8.53 and estimates of bin quality were generated with checkm v1.1.3. High-quality bins (greater than 90% est. completeness and less than 10% est. redundancy) were taxonomically classified with gtdb-tk v2.4.0 as MAGs (Metagenome-assembled genomes). Assemblies were created for each sample, but no gene calls were identified for NTC_3. Bins were recovered for only 5 samples. No MAGs were recovered.
Feature table decontamination and visualizations: All graphical displays were conducted using R v4.5.1. Bar plots and heatmaps were generated using R packages ggplot2 v3.5.1 and pheatmap v1.0.13, respectively. Taxonomy and function annotation tables from both read- and assembly-based processing steps were decontaminated with decontam v1.24.0, an R package designed to statistically identify contaminant features in a feature table. **

Note: Some reports contain output information for multiple columns. As such they are linked multiple times in the assay table under each relevant column but are only located under one of the shared directories." Samples were prepared using the Illumina Nextera XT DNA Library Preparation kit following the manufacturer's protocol. An equal volume of each sample library was used to create a library pool, which was sequenced on a MiSeq i100+ instrument at 2x159 cycles. The sample pool was balanced based on the results to create the final sample pool. The final loading concentration was 140 pmol.	Sequencing of the final DNA library pool was performed at DNA Services Facility at the Roy J. Carver Biotechnology Center at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. The final pool was sequenced on 1 lane of a XX flow cell on a NovaSeq X sequencer with the following set-up: R1:150, R2:150, i7:10, i5:10. BCL files were demultiplexed in BaseSpace using Dragen BCL-Convert 4.3.16.	Fastq file quality was assessed with FastQC v0.12.1 and reports were summarized with MultiQC v1.19. Read-based processing: Taxonomy assignment of decontaminated/human-removed reads was performed with three tools: Kraken v2.1.6, Kaiju v1.10.1, and MetaPhlAn v4.1.0. The resulting assignments from each tool were visualized as krona plots using krona v2.8.1.

Assembly-based processing: The decontaminated/human-removed reads were assembled with MEGAHIT v1.2.9. Genes were called with prodigal v2.6.3. Taxonomic classification of genes and contigs was performed with CAT v5.2.3. Functional annotation was done with KOFamScan v1.3.0. Reads were mapped to assemblies with bowtie2 v2.4.1, and coverage information was extracted for reads and contigs with samtools v1.20 and bbmap v38.86. Binning of contigs was performed with metabat2 v2.15. Bins were summarized with bit v1.8.53 and estimates of quality were generated with checkm v1.1.3. High-quality bins (greater than 90% est. completeness and less than 10% est. redundancy) were taxonomically classified with gtdb-tk v1.5.0 as MAGs (Metagenome-assembled genomes). Assemblies failed for 7 (of 32) samples: D1S7, D2S13, Buffer_AVE, NTC_5, NTC_6, NTC_7, and NTC_8.  Bins were recovered for 13 samples, and MAGs were recovered for 6 of these samples.
